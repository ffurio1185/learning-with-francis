{{ _("After a successful End Visit request, the application deletes that visit’s nickname, visitor row, answers, attempts, scores, progress, completion confirmations and learning/status history in one database transaction. {{ _("Simply reading an already open page, selecting an answer or filtering rows locally does not send a qualifying request. {{ _("Expired records are deleted at application startup and during subsequent learning or visit requests. Expiration is checked before activity renewal, so an expired visit cannot be revived.
